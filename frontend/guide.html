How To Use

Using the PharmaVision AI application is straightforward and user-friendly. To begin, simply upload a photo of the pill you wish to identify by clicking the "Upload" button on the main interface. Once the image is uploaded, the AI model will analyze it, using advanced machine learning algorithms to detect and recognize the pill. Within moments, the system will provide comprehensive information about the pill, including details on its composition, potential side effects, dosage recommendations, and other relevant data sourced through the Gemini API.

PharmaVision also includes a chatbot-like feature, where you can type in questions to receive additional details or clarification regarding the medication. This functionality allows users to inquire about specific concerns, such as possible interactions with other medications or any contraindications, ensuring that users have a complete understanding of the pill's properties. With its intuitive interface and responsive AI capabilities, PharmaVision makes pill identification and information retrieval both efficient and accessible, ultimately promoting safer medication use for its users.
